CUTLINE Quickstart Manual

Everything you need to customize, brand, and launch your luxury barbershop website in under 15 minutes. No coding required.

Open Live Website → Read Full Field Manual

1 Open & Preview Locally

CUTLINE is 100% static and requires no server installation or command-line build tools.

  1. Simply double-click index.html in your folder.
  2. The site will open instantly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
  3. You will see the complete luxury barbershop website with all photography, services, team members, and interactions functioning.

2 Personalize Your Business in config.js

Open config.js using any text editor (Notepad, TextEdit, VS Code). Look for the clearly commented sections:

A. Business Details & Contact: 
business: {
  name: "YOUR BARBERSHOP NAME",
  subname: "Master Barbers & Atelier",
  phone: "+1 (555) 123-4567",
  email: "hello@yourbarber.com",
  street: "123 Main Street, Suite 100",
  cityStateZip: "Your City, State / Province",
  bookingUrl: "https://your-square-or-fresha-link.com"
}
B. Linking Your Real Booking Engine:

CUTLINE works with all major salon & barbershop platforms. Paste your direct URL into bookingUrl:

  • Square Appointments: https://squareup.com/appointments/book/...
  • Fresha: https://fresha.com/a/...
  • Calendly: https://calendly.com/...
  • Acuity Scheduling: https://acuityscheduling.com/...
  • Booksy / Vagaro: Paste your direct shop link.
  • Built-in Modal: Leave as "#book-modal" to use the elegant built-in inquiry form!
C. Services & Prices:

Add, edit, or remove services from the services list in config.js. The menu automatically renders updated prices, descriptions, and durations!

3 Deploy to the Web for Free in 60 Seconds

Because CUTLINE is a clean static website, hosting is 100% free with enterprise-grade SSL and global CDNs:

Option 1: Netlify Drop (Easiest — No account required to test!)
  1. Visit app.netlify.com/drop in your browser.
  2. Drag and drop the entire cutline-barber-template folder into the designated box.
  3. Your website is instantly live with a secure HTTPS link!
  4. Connect your custom domain (e.g. mybarbershop.com) under Site Settings → Domain Management.
Option 2: Traditional Web Hosting (cPanel / GoDaddy / Bluehost / Namecheap)
  1. Log into your hosting cPanel and open File Manager.
  2. Navigate to public_html.
  3. Upload all files and folders (including index.html, config.js, css/, js/, assets/).
  4. Visit your domain name!
